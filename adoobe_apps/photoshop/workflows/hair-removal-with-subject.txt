CHANNEL-BASED HAIR MASK — STEP-BY-STEP
STEP 1 — Go to Channels Panel

Open Window → Channels

You will see:

RGB

Red

Green

Blue

Click each channel individually.

You are looking for:

✔ Dark hair
✔ Light background
✔ Maximum separation

For beach + sky images like yours, usually Blue channel has the most contrast.

STEP 2 — Duplicate the Best Channel

Drag that channel (likely Blue) to the New Channel icon

Rename it: Hair Mask

Now you are working on a grayscale image.

STEP 3 — Increase Contrast (Critical Step)

Press:

Ctrl/Cmd + L → Opens Levels

Goal:

Hair = pure black

Background = pure white

Move:

Black slider right

White slider left

Midtones slightly adjust

DO NOT crush the fine hair strands.

Work slowly.

Click OK.

STEP 4 — Clean Manually With Brush

Now refine manually.

Press B for Brush.

Settings:

Soft round brush

Opacity 100%

Flow 100%

Do this:

• Paint BLACK over solid subject areas (face, clothes, body)
• Paint WHITE over obvious background
• Zoom 200–300% for hair

Important:
Do NOT overpaint flyaway strands.

If needed:
Use a smaller soft brush at 50% opacity for delicate strands.

STEP 5 — Refine with Dodge & Burn (Advanced Control)

This is optional but powerful.

Select:

Dodge Tool (Highlights) → brighten background

Burn Tool (Shadows) → darken hair

Use low Exposure (5–10%)

This enhances micro contrast in strands without destroying them.

STEP 6 — Load Channel as Selection

Hold:

Ctrl/Cmd + Click on the channel thumbnail

You now have a selection.

Important:
White = selected.

If selection is reversed:

Shift + Ctrl/Cmd + I to invert.

STEP 7 — Convert to Layer Mask

Go back to Layers panel

Select your image layer

Click Add Layer Mask

Boom — you now have a precise luminance-based mask.

CLEANING HALOS (Backlit Issue Fix)

Because your image is backlit, you'll likely see light glow edges.

To fix:

Option A — Mask Edge Blur

Click the mask

Go to:
Filter → Blur → Gaussian Blur

Set:
0.3 – 0.6px

Then:
Image → Adjustments → Levels
Adjust slightly to tighten edge.

Option B — Apply Mask Edge Shift

Go to:
Properties Panel → Mask → Shift Edge

Set:

-2% to -5%

OPTIONAL: Defringe

Layer → Matting → Defringe
1–2px max.

Why This Works Better Than Refine Edge

Refine Edge:

Uses edge detection + AI estimation

Fails in low-contrast backlight

Channel method:

Uses actual pixel luminance

Preserves fine transparency

Maintains realistic hair falloff

This is what professional compositors use in fashion/advertising retouching.
